Field notes

  • Compass off? Tap the compass and draw a slow figure-8 with the phone, away from metal.
  • No magnetic mounts or cases. A magnet next to the phone bends the compass for every shot.
  • From a car, shoot through an open window with the phone held out; the body and engine pull the compass. The heading is always the camera's, never the road's.
  • GPS: open sky helps; buildings, trees and car roofs hurt. Shots above ± 30 m are blocked.
  • Tilt between 40° and 80° from straight down keeps the crosshair point reliable.
  • Video logs position, direction and tilt every second. Keep the app on screen while filming: locking the phone or switching apps stops the clip (what was filmed is kept). Tap a type while filming to mark that moment.
