CropView

Geo-tagged field capture with camera direction & FOV mapping for crop growth stage analysis.

Requires: Camera · GPS · Compass

Walk the field and capture a shot per crop — each one is geotagged with position, heading and ground footprint, and tagged to a growth stage. When you're done, export the session as a ZIP from the Data tab.

🔬 Purpose

Mobile geo-tagged field capture app correlating phone camera POV with Sentinel-2 satellite imagery for FAO-56 crop growth stage validation using Kc coefficients.

Open Source GitHub Pages FAO-56 Kc Sentinel-2 GeoJSON IIT Bombay

🧭 What's new in v3

The heading pipeline no longer trusts a single browser event. It watches its own sensor stream, throws out invalid readings instead of quietly defaulting to North, notices when a phone-specific bug freezes the compass, and silently resubscribes and recovers. If it truly can't get a fix, it says so instead of showing you a confident, wrong cone. Tap the compass any time to force a recalibration.

Since v3.2: the heading is now always where the camera points, never the direction you are travelling — so side-looking shots from a vehicle are recorded correctly, and the map cone follows the phone live, even when held upright.
